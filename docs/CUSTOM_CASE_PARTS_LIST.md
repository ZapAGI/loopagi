## Approach: Aluminum Extrusion Frame

The most versatile approach for a custom case is **2020 aluminum extrusion**
(T-slot / V-slot). This is the same system used in 3D printers and CNC
machines. It's modular, strong, easy to cut, and infinitely adjustable.

**Why 2020 extrusion:**
- No welding or sheet metal bending required
- Infinitely adjustable — slide components along rails
- Strong enough for heavy GPUs (RTX 5080 weighs ~1.5kg)
- Clean industrial aesthetic
- Easy to add/remove components later
- Compatible with 3D-printed brackets and adapters
